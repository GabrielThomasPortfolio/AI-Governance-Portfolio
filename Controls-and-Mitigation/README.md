**Governance Controls Framework**

This folder contains the governance layer controls required to maintain safe system behavior.

Controls include:
* Intent classification
* Refusal & boundary enforcement
* Safe alternative generation
* Multi step reasoning safety
* External knowledge governance
* Drift monitoring
* Logging & oversight
* Explainability
* Context sensitivity
* Safety consistency
* Tiered escalation pathways

These controls align with ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act and form the backbone of the system’s safety posture.
