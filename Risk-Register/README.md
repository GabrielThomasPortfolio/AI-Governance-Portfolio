**AI Risk Register**
This folder contains the AI Risk Register for a general‑purpose help chatbot.
The register translates harmful outputs into structured risks with:

* Harm domains
* Business impact
* Likelihood and impact scoring
* Residual risk
* ISO 42001 control mapping
* EU AI Act regulatory exposure
* Drift vectors and monitoring requirements
* Failure‑mode signatures

The risk register is designed to be audit‑ready and suitable for enterprise governance programs.
